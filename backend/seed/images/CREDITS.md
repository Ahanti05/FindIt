## Generated by `make_demo_images.py`

- **idcard.jpg** – a fictional ID card drawn from scratch (no real person).
- **\*_reshot.jpg** – a second "photo" of the same object made by re-cropping,
  rotating, warping and re-lighting the original. These simulate the finder
  photographing the owner's item, and are the demo's true matches. Real-world
  accuracy should be measured with genuinely separate photos.
